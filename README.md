# KRIDALEELA 🇮🇳

### Discover • Play • Learn • Preserve

KRIDALEELA is a game-first digital platform designed to preserve and promote Indian traditional, regional, historical, and cultural games through interactive digital experiences.

The platform brings traditional games and cultural stories into a modern, accessible digital environment where users can discover, play, learn, and request games from different regions of India.

---

## 🎯 Problem

Many Indian traditional games and cultural practices are closely connected with local history, communities, languages, and traditions.

However:

- Younger generations have fewer opportunities to discover and experience many traditional games.
- Regional games and cultural knowledge are often fragmented across communities and offline sources.
- Different regions may have different rules and variations of the same game.
- Traditional games can become difficult to preserve when their rules, context, and cultural origins are not documented.
- Existing digital gaming platforms do not primarily focus on preserving India's regional and traditional games.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

KRIDALEELA provides a centralized digital platform where users can:

- Discover Indian traditional and cultural games.
- Play selected games digitally.
- Learn the rules and cultural context behind each game.
- Explore cultural stories and related content.
- Request regional games that they want to see on the platform.
- Discover games from different Indian regions and languages.

The platform initially develops and publishes games itself. User requests help identify which traditional games should be explored and developed next.

---

## 🎮 Current Games

The prototype focuses on implementing traditional games with clearly documented rule variants.

### Chenne Mane

A traditional South Indian seed-sowing board game represented using a 2 × 7 pit board.

The digital implementation focuses on:

- Two-player gameplay
- Seed distribution
- Turn management
- Chain sowing
- Capture rules
- Game-state management
- Win-condition detection
- Interactive SVG board

### Chausar

A traditional Indian cross-shaped board game.

The prototype uses a clearly defined traditional-style ruleset rather than attempting to represent every historical variation.

## 📖 Cultural Stories

KRIDALEELA also provides a separate cultural story section.

Possible formats include:

- Illustrated stories
- Slide-based stories
- Audio stories
- Video stories
- Comic-style stories
- Text-based stories

Possible categories include:

- Ajji Kathe
- Folklore
- Historical stories
- Regional stories
- Biography
- Mythology
- Fiction
- Horror
- Children's stories
- Festival and cultural stories

Stories are treated separately from games while remaining part of the larger cultural preservation ecosystem.
